# Take the AATG practice test

## Purpose

How a student goes from a paper password slip to starting the AATG
practice test on Lingco. These steps assume nothing goes wrong. If
something does, see [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

## Prerequisites

- The student is already registered with Lingco. See
  [Exam registration](index.md).
- The student brings their own:
    - Laptop (Windows, Mac, or Chromebook) with a recent Chrome, Firefox,
      or Safari browser. No VPN.
    - Headphones or headset.
- The teacher brings the paper slips, one per student. Each slip has the
  student's `student.gssb.org` email address, email password, and Lingco
  password.

## Steps

1. **Get your paper slip** with your passwords from the teacher.
2. **Get your laptop ready**, before anything else. If something here
   doesn't work, tell a teacher right away; see
   [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).
    1. **Test your audio.** Plug in your headset and play any sound or
       video.
    2. **Turn off translation tools.** Google Translate, translation
       browser extensions, and built-in browser translation can trigger
       Lingco's anti-cheat protection and **get you disqualified**.
3. **Open your school email.**
    1. Go to [office.com](https://www.office.com/).
    2. Sign in with the **email address** and **email password** from the
       slip.
    3. Open `Outlook`.
4. **Confirm your Lingco account.** Your Lingco account isn't created
   until you do this. You usually have to click `Confirm email` twice, in
   two different emails.
    1. Lingco sent you two emails: one to confirm your account and one
       inviting you to the practice test. Open the one with the subject
       `Confirm email address` and click `Confirm email`.
    2. That link has usually expired by now. A new tab opens and says
       Lingco sent you a new confirmation email. Go back to the Outlook
       tab.
    3. Wait for the new email with the same subject, then click
       `Confirm email` in it.
    4. Your Lingco account is now confirmed.
5. **Join the course.** In the tab that opened, click the big blue
   `Join course` button.
    - No `Join course` button? Go to
      [class.lingco.io](https://class.lingco.io/) and sign in with your
      email address and the **Lingco password** from the slip.
6. **Fill out the questionnaire.** Click `Start questionnaire`, near the
   bottom right, then answer all the questions.
7. **Start the practice test.**
    1. Click `Dashboard` in the menu on the left.
    2. Near where you found `Start questionnaire`, click the button to
       start the practice test.

## Troubleshooting

??? question "Student can't sign in to email (changed and forgot the password)"

    Some students changed their email password and forgot the new one. Ask
    the IT team to reset the email password back to the one on the slip,
    then sign in again.

??? question "“You can't access this resource” when signing in to Office"

    1. On another computer (for example, a GSSB Chromebook), do steps 3
       and 4 with the student's slip: sign in to Outlook and confirm the
       Lingco account.
    2. Sign out of Outlook on that computer.
    3. On the student's computer, go to
       [class.lingco.io](https://class.lingco.io/) and sign in with the
       `student.gssb.org` email address and the **Lingco password** from
       the slip.

??? question "Office sign-in is stuck"

    1. Close all browser tabs and windows, then open the browser again and
       start at [office.com](https://www.office.com/).
    2. Still stuck? Do the Office sign-in in a private window
       (`Incognito` in Chrome, `InPrivate` in Edge, `Private Window` in
       Safari). Press `Ctrl+Shift+N` (Windows, Chromebook) or
       `Cmd+Shift+N` (Mac).

??? question "No `Join course` button after confirming the account"

    Go to [class.lingco.io](https://class.lingco.io/) and sign in with your
    `student.gssb.org` email address and the **Lingco password** from the
    slip (not the email password).

??? question "Student can't sign in to Lingco (password doesn't work)"

    1. Check that the student is using the **Lingco password** from the
       slip, not the email password.
    2. Still not working? Type the Lingco password again, but leave out
       `gssb` at the start.

??? question "No audio"

    Test audio as early as possible so there's time to fix it.

    1. Try a GSSB headset.
    2. Still no audio? Move the student to a GSSB laptop.

??? question "Turn off translation tools"

    Translation tools can trigger anti-cheat protection and disqualify the
    student.

    - **Chrome:** open `Settings` > `Languages` and turn off
      `Use Google Translate`.
    - **Edge:** open `Settings` > `Languages` and turn off
      `Offer to translate pages that aren't in a language I read`.
    - **Extensions:** turn off any translation extension (for example,
      Google Translate) in `Extensions`.
    - Close any translation app or website.

??? question "Student forgot their headset"

    Give them a GSSB headset. They can keep it.

??? question "Student forgot their computer"

    Give them a GSSB Chromebook.

??? question "Computer can't connect to the school Wi-Fi"

    Older computers that only support 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi can't connect; the
    school no longer offers a 2.4 GHz network. Give the student a GSSB
    Chromebook.

??? question "Browser is too old or not supported"

    Lingco needs a recent version of Chrome, Firefox, or Safari.

    1. Check whether another supported browser is already installed on the
       computer, and use that.
    2. If not, give the student a GSSB Chromebook (or, if there's time,
       install Chrome).

??? question "Lingco didn't save the student's progress"

    Lingco sometimes doesn't save a student's answers correctly. The
    principal can reset that part of the test from the Lingco
    administrator account. Tell the principal the student's name and which
    part of the test to reset.

??? question "Student's laptop has a VPN turned on"

    Students can't use a VPN for the practice test.

    1. Turn off the VPN in its app. Look for its icon in the taskbar
       (Windows), the menu bar (Mac), or the status area (Chromebook).
    2. Turn off any VPN extension in the browser's `Extensions`.
    3. Can't turn it off (for example, a work laptop)? Give the student a
       GSSB Chromebook.
