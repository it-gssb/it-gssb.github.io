## Symptoms

- You click **Print** and nothing comes out.
- The printer is missing from the list when you try to print.
- Windows says the printer is *Offline* or shows an error.
- Documents are stuck in the queue and newer ones don't print either.

## Quick fixes

1. **Check the printer itself.** It's switched on, has paper, and the
   display doesn't show an error or a paper jam.
2. **Check the Wi-Fi.** Your laptop must be on the school Wi-Fi, not the
   guest network.
3. **Pick the right printer.** In the print window, check the printer name
   under **Printer** matches the label on the printer.
4. **Restart the printer.** Turn it off, wait 30 seconds, turn it on again,
   and wait until it shows *Ready* (about two minutes).

## Step-by-step fix

If the quick fixes didn't help:

1. **Clear the print queue.** Open **Start → Settings → Bluetooth & devices
   → Printers & scanners**, select the printer, click **Open print queue**,
   then cancel all documents.
2. **Remove and add the printer again.** On the same settings page, select
   the printer and click **Remove**. Then click **Add device**, wait for the
   printer to appear, and click **Add device** next to it.
3. **Print a test page.** Select the printer, click **Print a test page**,
   and confirm it comes out of the right printer.
